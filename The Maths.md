
## What actually happens — step by step

**Step 1 — You buy the shares**

```
500 shares × ₹1,500  =  ₹7,50,000 paid
```

**Step 2 — You sell the 1550 call and collect premium**

```
₹25 × 500  =  ₹12,500 received
```

Feels good. Money in the account today.

**Step 3 — Reliance runs to ₹1,700**

Your strike was 1550. The stock is above it. The buyer exercises.

**Step 4 — Your shares are taken away at 1550**

```
500 shares × ₹1,550  =  ₹7,75,000 received
```

**Step 5 — Add it all up**

```
From selling shares      ₹7,75,000
From the premium         ₹  12,500
                        -----------
Total in hand            ₹7,87,500
Minus what you paid      ₹7,50,000
                        -----------
PROFIT                   ₹  37,500   ✓ green
```

₹37,500 profit. Your statement is happy. You did nothing wrong.

## Now the part nobody shows you

**Step 6 — What if you had just done nothing?**

```
500 shares × ₹1,700  =  ₹8,50,000
Minus what you paid     ₹7,50,000
                       -----------
                        ₹1,00,000
```

```
Doing nothing        ₹1,00,000
Covered call         ₹  37,500
                    -----------
You gave up          ₹  62,500
```

**Step 7 — Now try to buy your shares back**

This is the step that matters. You wanted these shares for your children. So you must buy them again — at today's price of ₹1,700.

```
Money you have           ₹7,87,500
Price per share          ₹    1,700
                        -----------
Shares you can buy              463
```

```
You started with     500 shares
You now have         463 shares
                    ------------
YOU LOST              37 shares
```

**Step 8 — And tax hasn't even come yet**

```
Gain on shares    (1550−1500) × 500 = ₹25,000  → LTCG @12.5%  = ₹3,125
Premium income                        ₹12,500  → slab @30%    = ₹3,750
                                                              ---------
                                                Tax           = ₹6,875
```

```
₹7,87,500 − ₹6,875  =  ₹7,80,625
₹7,80,625 ÷ ₹1,700  =  459 shares
```

**Final position: 459 shares. You started with 500. You lost 41 shares — and your statement said you made ₹37,500.**
