## Starting position — ₹10,00,000 capital

**Step 1 — Buy one lot of Reliance**

```
500 shares × ₹1,500   =  ₹7,50,000
Cash left over        =  ₹2,50,000
                        -----------
Total capital            ₹10,00,000
```

**Step 2 — Sell the 1550 call, collect premium**

```
₹25 × 500  =  ₹12,500 received
```

**Step 3 — Reliance runs to ₹1,700. You get assigned at 1550.**

```
500 × ₹1,550  =  ₹7,75,000 received
```

**Step 4 — Count your money**

```
Shares sold at 1550      ₹7,75,000
Premium kept             ₹  12,500
Cash you already had     ₹2,50,000
                        -----------
TOTAL                    ₹10,37,500
```

**Step 5 — You made ₹37,500.** Statement is green. Feels like a good month.

---

## Step 6 — Now compare with doing absolutely nothing

If you had never sold that call:

```
500 shares × ₹1,700      ₹8,50,000
Cash you already had     ₹2,50,000
                        -----------
TOTAL                    ₹11,00,000
```

```
Doing nothing            ₹11,00,000
Covered call             ₹10,37,500
                        -----------
YOU ARE BEHIND BY        ₹   62,500
```

So your effective capital is **₹10,00,000 − ₹62,500** compared to where you should have been.

---

## Step 7 — Try to get your shares back

You wanted these shares for your children. So buy them again at ₹1,700.

```
You have                 ₹10,37,500
Buy 500 × ₹1,700       − ₹ 8,50,000
                        -----------
Cash left                ₹ 1,87,500
```

```
Cash you started with    ₹2,50,000
Cash you have now        ₹1,87,500
                        -----------
GONE                     ₹  62,500
```

**Same 500 shares. ₹62,500 less cash.**

---

## Step 8 — And tax comes on top

```
Gain on shares  (1550−1500) × 500 = ₹25,000  → LTCG 12.5%  = ₹3,125
Premium income                      ₹12,500  → slab 30%    = ₹3,750
                                                            --------
                                              TAX            ₹6,875
```

```
Cash left after rebuy    ₹1,87,500
Minus tax              − ₹    6,875
                        -----------
                         ₹1,80,625
```

**Final: 500 shares + ₹1,80,625. You started with 500 shares + ₹2,50,000.**

**₹69,375 gone — and your statement said you made ₹37,500.**
